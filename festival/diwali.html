Before you play this Diwali

Is Dhamaka Blocks free?

Yes, it is free to download and play on Android. It contains banner and interstitial ads, plus optional rewarded ads.

Can I play offline?

The core block puzzle works offline after installation. Online features and optional rewarded ads need connectivity when used.

Is there an in-game Diwali event?

This page celebrates Diwali with the game and a greeting card. It does not advertise a special in-game festival event or bonus.

How do I share the greeting card?

Choose "Save the card" above to download it. Send the saved image with your own Diwali wishes, or use "Share on WhatsApp" to share this page.

Which devices can play?

The app is available on Android through Google Play. This page is not a browser-playable game.
