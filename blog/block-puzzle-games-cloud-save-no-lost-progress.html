Why Do Block Puzzle Games Lose Progress When You Switch Phones?

Most simple block puzzle games save your score, coins, and unlocks only to local storage on the device you're playing on, so progress disappears the moment you uninstall, factory-reset, or move to a new phone. A local save has no idea an account exists — it's just a file sitting in that app's storage, and a fresh install starts that file from zero.

An OS-level device backup (the kind your phone can take automatically) can sometimes restore that local save file, but only if app-data backup was switched on beforehand and you sign back into the exact same device account afterward. It doesn't follow you to a different platform, a factory-fresh setup where you skip the restore step, or a phone where backup was never enabled in the first place — which is why it still catches people out.

What Should You Check Before Trusting a Block Puzzle Game's Save System?

Check whether the save is tied to a real account before you assume your progress is safe — a few minutes in Settings tells you more than any store listing. Look for these four things:

  • Does it require signing in to an account (like Google Play Games), rather than just storing a local profile on the device?
  • Does switching devices merge your progress or overwrite it? A system that silently overwrites can erase progress you already had on the device you're switching away from.
  • What exactly syncs? Some games only sync a single high-score number; others sync your full state — coins, streaks, and unlocked cosmetics included.
  • Are there achievements or leaderboards? Their presence is a useful signal that the save system is actually wired into platform account infrastructure, rather than just a local flag with a friendly label.

How Does Cloud Save Work in Dhamaka Blocks?

Dhamaka Blocks syncs your progress through an optional Google Play Games sign-in — available from Settings, or auto-detected if it's already set up on your device — and once you're signed in, your best score, coin balance, daily reward streak, and unlocked board themes all follow your account to any device.

How merging actually works

Signing in on a second device never blanket-overwrites what's already there. For each field — best score, coins, streak, themes — Dhamaka Blocks keeps whichever value is higher or most recently changed. There's one save slot per account, and there's no manual conflict-resolution screen yet; the merge is automatic.

What About Achievements and Leaderboards?

Signing in with Google Play Games also unlocks 18 achievements and two leaderboards — Best Score and Longest Streak — both reachable from Settings → Play Games. Like cloud save itself, all three are entirely optional; the game is fully playable offline and signed out, and nothing is required to keep playing without them.
