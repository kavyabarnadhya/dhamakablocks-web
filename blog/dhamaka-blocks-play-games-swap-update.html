Play Games Achievements & the SWAP Booster Have Arrived

Dhamaka Blocks now talks to Google Play Games. Sign in and your progress starts earning real achievements — 18 of them, spanning score milestones, combo streaks, booster usage and daily-streak dedication. Alongside that, a fourth booster joins the lineup: SWAP, for the exact moment one bad tray piece is the only thing standing between you and a longer run.

  • Play Games sign-in — optional, one tap in Settings, tracks your achievement progress on your Google account
  • 18 achievements — first game, score milestones, combo tiers, booster usage, board clears and daily streaks
  • SWAP booster — 35 coins to trade exactly one tray piece without touching the other two
  • Feel upgrades — near-miss moments now get their own bespoke audio and haptic treatment

Sign In With Play Games

If you already have Google Play Games set up on your device, Dhamaka Blocks picks that up automatically — no extra tap needed. If you don't, there's now a sign-in tile in Settings you can use whenever you like. Either way, it's entirely your choice: nothing is sent anywhere until you're signed in, and the game is just as fully playable offline and signed out as it always has been.

Why sign in?

Once you're signed in, your achievement unlocks are tracked against your Google account instead of just this one install — so they follow you if you switch devices, and you can compare your Play Games profile with friends.

The full achievement list is built from milestones the game already tracks — nothing you have to learn from scratch, just recognition for things you were probably doing anyway.

That's 18 in total, and every one of them unlocks off events the game was already firing — you don't need to change how you play, just sign in and let them accumulate.

Meet SWAP — the Fourth Booster

REFRESH deals you a brand new tray of 3. That's great when the whole tray is bad — but it also throws away the two pieces you might have been counting on to extend a combo. SWAP fixes that: it only ever touches one slot.

BoosterCostWhat It Does
UNDO25 coinsReverses your most recent placement
SWAP35 coinsTap to arm, then tap one tray slot — only that piece is replaced
REFRESH40 coinsDiscards your current tray for 3 fresh pieces
BOMB60 coinsTap to arm, then tap a filled cell to clear a 3×3 area around it

Tap SWAP to arm it — that part's free — then tap the one tray piece that's actually the problem. The other two stay exactly where they were, so any setup you've already built around them survives. Like the other three boosters, SWAP is also available during the brief "last chance" window if a game would otherwise end.

Also in This Update

Near-miss moments — a piece that only just fits, a combo that's one placement from breaking — now get their own bespoke audio and haptic feedback rather than a generic cue, for UNDO, REFRESH, level-ups and NEW BEST celebrations alike. As always, none of that shows up as a visual hint on the grid itself — Dhamaka Blocks stays a game about reading the board yourself. This release also ships accessibility improvements to loading and share-spinner semantics, and several under-the-hood security hardenings.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Play Games sign-in do in Dhamaka Blocks?

It links your session to your Google Play Games profile so your achievement progress is tracked on your Google account. It's entirely optional — the game is fully playable signed out.

How many achievements does Dhamaka Blocks have?

18 achievements across six categories: a first-game milestone, four score thresholds, five combo tiers, four booster-usage badges, one full-board-clear badge, and three daily-streak milestones.

What is the SWAP booster and how much does it cost?

SWAP costs 35 coins. Arm it, then tap one tray slot to trade just that piece for a fresh one — the other two pieces in your tray are left untouched, unlike REFRESH which replaces all three.
