Is Dhamaka Blocks Free, Safe & Offline?

Yes to all three: Dhamaka Blocks is free to install, safe to download from the official Google Play Store, and its core puzzle gameplay works fully offline. Here's the detail behind each of those answers, so you can check for yourself rather than take our word for it.

See for yourself — it's a free download either way.

Get the Game — Free

Is Dhamaka Blocks free?

Yes — Dhamaka Blocks is free to install and free to play on Android, with no purchase required to download the game or play the core 8×8 block puzzle. You earn coins just by playing (roughly 1 coin per 100 points scored, capped at 50 coins per game) plus a 7-day escalating Daily Reward, and those coins can be spent on optional boosters (UNDO, REFRESH, BOMB) or cosmetic board themes if you want them — none of that is required to enjoy the core game.

Is Dhamaka Blocks safe to download?

Yes — Dhamaka Blocks is distributed exclusively through the official Google Play Store under the app ID com.dhamakagames.dhamakablocks, built by Dhamaka Games, an Indian studio. That means it goes through Google's standard Play Store review and update process, rather than being sideloaded from an unofficial source. You don't need to create an account or hand over personal information to play, and the game's data practices are laid out in a public Privacy Policy you can read before installing.

If you're comparing Dhamaka Blocks to other options, the safest general rule for any block puzzle app is the same one that applies here: install only from the official Play Store listing, and skip anything claiming to be a "modded" or "hacked" version of a game — those aren't official and carry real risk.

Does Dhamaka Blocks work offline?

Yes — the core puzzle gameplay works fully offline once the game is installed. Placing pieces, clearing rows and columns, scoring, the combo multiplier, difficulty progression, and the daily coin reward all function without an internet connection. You could install it on Wi-Fi and then play the entire game in airplane mode.

What Android permissions does it ask for?

Dhamaka Blocks requests a short, standard list of permissions — none of which are required for the puzzle itself to run. The table below breaks down what each one does.

Permission What It's Used For
INTERNETLoading advertisements
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEChecking connectivity before attempting to load an ad
VIBRATEOptional haptic feedback during gameplay
Advertising ID (AD_ID)Ad personalisation — resettable/opt-out via Android Settings → Google → Ads

Notice that every one of those is tied to ads or optional feedback, not to the puzzle logic — which is exactly why the core game keeps working with no connection at all.

What data does Dhamaka Blocks collect?

Very little, and almost none of it leaves your device. Your best score, daily play streak, and sound/vibration preferences are stored locally on your device using Android's standard local storage and are never sent to Dhamaka Games' servers. The app is ad-supported, so ad and analytics providers may collect standard device/usage data as described in the full Privacy Policy — that page also covers your rights under India's DPDP Act and, for EU/EEA users, the GDPR.
