How the Daily Reward Streak Works in Dhamaka Blocks

The Daily Reward in Dhamaka Blocks pays out on a 7-day escalating cycle — claim once a day from the home screen and each day pays more coins than the last, topping out at 400 coins on Day 7 before the cycle restarts. Miss a day and the streak resets to Day 1, but a separate lifetime counter still keeps track of your total claims for milestone chests. Here's exactly how both systems work.

Claiming your Daily Reward takes seconds — open the app and tap the home screen once a day.

What Is the Daily Reward Streak?

It's a coin bonus you claim once per day from Dhamaka Blocks' home screen, on top of whatever coins you earn from actually playing. The payout escalates the longer your streak runs, which rewards opening the game daily even on days you only have time for a quick round or two.

The 7-Day Reward Cycle

Each day you claim pays more than the day before, with Day 7 delivering the biggest single payout in the cycle before it loops back to Day 1.

Streak Day Coins Awarded
Day 125 coins
Day 240 coins
Day 360 coins
Day 480 coins

Claim all 7 days without missing one and you collect 885 coins from the Daily Reward alone — before counting anything earned in-game. After Day 7, the cycle starts over from Day 1.

What Happens If You Miss a Day?

Missing a day resets your streak back to Day 1. If you were on Day 5 and skip a day, your next claim pays 25 coins again rather than picking up where you left off — the escalating ladder only holds for an unbroken run of daily claims.

Good to know

A reset only affects the 7-day payout ladder. It does not erase progress toward the lifetime milestone chests below — those keep counting regardless of streak breaks.

Lifetime Milestone Chests (Separate From the Streak)

Alongside the 7-day cycle, Dhamaka Blocks tracks your lifetime total of daily-reward claims and unlocks one-time bonus chests at three thresholds. These totals accumulate across your entire play history and don't reset even if your current streak breaks.

Lifetime Daily Claims Chest Reward
7 total days150 coins
30 total days400 coins + free Aurora theme
100 total days800 coins + free Monsoon theme

The Aurora and Monsoon themes are two of the few ways to unlock a board theme without spending coins directly — Aurora, in fact, can only be obtained through the 30-day milestone chest; it isn't purchasable any other way.

What Coins From the Daily Reward Are Good For

Daily Reward coins go into the same balance as coins earned from playing (roughly 1 coin per 100 points scored, capped at 50 coins per game), and they can be spent on mid-game boosters — UNDO (30 coins), REFRESH (40 coins), and BOMB (60 coins) — or on unlocking board themes. A single Day 7 claim alone covers several boosters, or a meaningful chunk of a Signature-tier theme.

Tips to Protect Your Streak

  • Claim early in your session. Open the app and tap the Daily Reward before you start a round, so a busy day doesn't cause you to forget.
  • Remember Day 7 is worth protecting most. Missing a day right before your 400-coin payout resets you all the way back to 25 coins — plan around it if you can.
  • Don't stress over an occasional miss. Your lifetime milestone progress toward the 7/30/100-day chests keeps accumulating even after a streak reset, so nothing you've already banked is lost.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long is the Dhamaka Blocks Daily Reward cycle?

The Daily Reward runs on a 7-day cycle. Coin payouts escalate each day you claim: 25, 40, 60, 80, 120, 160, and 400 coins on Day 7, after which the cycle starts over from Day 1.

What happens if I miss a day?

Missing a day resets your Daily Reward streak back to Day 1, so your next claim pays out 25 coins instead of wherever you left off. The 7-day payout ladder does not carry over across a missed day.

Do the lifetime milestone chests reset if my streak breaks?

No. Milestone chests track your lifetime total of daily-reward claims, separately from the current 7-day streak. Even if a missed day resets you to Day 1, your lifetime claim count keeps climbing toward the next milestone.

How many coins can I earn from one full Daily Reward cycle?

Claiming all 7 days of one cycle (25 + 40 + 60 + 80 + 120 + 160 + 400) totals 885 coins, on top of anything earned separately at the end of each game.
