How Combo Multipliers Work in Block Puzzle Games

A combo multiplier rewards you for clearing a line on consecutive turns in a row, multiplying the bonus points from each clear the longer your streak runs. In Dhamaka Blocks, that streak climbs through six tiers, from a 1.0× baseline up to a 10.0× multiplier at 6 or more consecutive clearing turns — the single biggest lever in the entire scoring system.

What Is a Combo Multiplier?

A combo multiplier is a scaling factor applied to your line-clear bonus that grows for every consecutive turn you manage to clear at least one row or column. It's a common mechanic across the block puzzle genre — the exact tiers and payouts vary by game, but the underlying idea is the same: reward players who keep the board flowing instead of stalling on single, isolated clears.

The multiplier doesn't touch every point you earn. It applies specifically to the line-clear bonus for that turn — the extra points you get for completing a row or column, on top of the base points you already earned just for placing the piece.

How Do You Build and Keep a Combo Streak?

You build a streak by clearing at least one line on every turn, back to back, with no gaps. Each additional consecutive clearing turn pushes you to the next tier in the multiplier table below, and the streak keeps growing for as long as you keep clearing — there's no upper limit on how many turns in a row you can chain.

It doesn't matter whether a single turn clears one line or five — what matters for the streak counter is whether that turn cleared anything at all. A modest single-row clear on turn 6 of a streak is worth just as much toward maintaining your multiplier as a spectacular quad clear.

What Happens If You Miss a Clear?

Missing a clear resets your streak back down to a 1.0× multiplier — the very next line-clear bonus you earn starts the count over from tier one. Your total score is never rolled back; only the multiplier applied to future bonuses resets, so a broken streak costs you future potential, not points you've already banked.

Good to know

A turn where you place all 3 pieces from the tray without completing a single row or column counts as a miss, even if the board still has open space. The streak only cares about whether a line actually cleared.

Combo Multiplier vs Line-Clear Bonus — How They Stack

The multiplier and the line-clear bonus stack multiplicatively, not additively — you take the base bonus for the number of lines cleared that turn and multiply it by your current streak tier. In Dhamaka Blocks, clearing 1 line pays a +10 point bonus, 2 lines at once pays +35, 3 lines pays +75, 4 lines pays +130, and 5 or more lines pays +200 — all before the multiplier is applied.

Put the two tables together and the payoff becomes obvious: a 3-line clear (+75 bonus) on turn 1 of a streak is worth 75 points, but that same 3-line clear on turn 6 of an active streak is worth 750 points from the bonus alone — a 10× jump for the identical move, just because the streak was already rolling.

How Do You Keep the Streak Alive on Purpose?

You keep a streak alive by managing the board so that a clear is available on every turn, not just planning one move at a time. A few habits that help:

  • Look at all 3 tray pieces before placing any of them — sequence your placements so the last piece you place is the one that completes a line, rather than accidentally clearing early and leaving nothing for the remaining pieces.
  • Keep at least one row or column close to full at all times, so a small piece can finish it off even on an awkward turn.
  • Don't chase a big multi-line clear at the cost of missing a turn entirely — a guaranteed single-line clear that keeps the streak alive is usually worth more than gambling on a bigger clear a turn or two later.
  • Use a REFRESH booster if the current tray genuinely can't clear anything, rather than letting the streak break on a turn you could have avoided.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all block puzzle games have combo multipliers?

Not all of them, but a streak-based multiplier on consecutive clearing turns is a common mechanic across the genre. The exact tiers and payouts vary by game — Dhamaka Blocks uses a six-tier scale from 1.0× up to 10.0×.

What's the highest combo multiplier in Dhamaka Blocks?

10.0×, reached at 6 or more consecutive clearing turns. It applies to the line-clear bonus for that turn, not to your base placement points.

Does missing one clear reset my entire score?

No. Missing a clear only resets your combo streak back to a 1.0× multiplier on future line-clear bonuses — every point you've already scored stays on the board.

Do combo multipliers apply to base placement points too?

No. In Dhamaka Blocks, the multiplier applies only to the line-clear bonus for that turn — the 1 point per cell you earn for simply placing a piece is unaffected.

Is there a cap on how long a combo streak can go?

There's no cap on the streak length itself, but the multiplier stops climbing at 10.0× once you reach 6 consecutive clearing turns — turn 7, 8, and beyond all pay out at the same 10.0× rate as long as the streak continues.
